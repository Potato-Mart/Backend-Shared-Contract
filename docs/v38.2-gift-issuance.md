# v38.2.0 gift-card issuance adoption

This additive release preserves module `/v38` and the legacy purchase-only `GiftCardIssuedEvent` v1. `pubsub/pricing.GiftCardIssuedEventV2` is the current payload for `wallet.gift_card_issued` **event_version v2**. The v2 fact accurately covers purchase, membership reward and refund replacement. It reuses Pricing's existing `customer-events` publication and Notification consumer route; it adds no topic or event type.

## Exact shape

```json
{"issuance_id":"issuance-1","source":"membership_reward","issued_value":{"amount_minor":5500,"currency":"AUD"},"market_code":"market-au","country_code":"AU","issued_at":"2026-09-29T00:00:00Z"}
```

| Field | Requiredness | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `issuance_id` | Required string | Immutable Pricing-owned issuance identity; also the envelope aggregate/ordering key and private lookup correlation |
| `source` | Required `GiftCardIssuanceSource` | `purchase`, `membership_reward` or `refund_replacement` |
| `issued_value` | Required Money | Original total credited initial value in its currency, frozen at issuance |
| `market_code` | Optional string | Authoritative owning market; absence is no evidence, never permission to infer one |
| `country_code` | Optional CountryCode | Authoritative owning country |
| `issued_at` | Required timestamp | Time the issuance fact was committed |

`issued_value` equals the card's initial credited value, including a purchase bonus when one exists. It is **not** the amount charged to a buyer, a later spendable balance, the refund payment amount, points spent, or purchase denomination evidence. For example, a purchased card charged at AUD 50 with AUD 5 bonus carries issued_value AUD 55. A reward issued at AUD 55 also carries AUD 55 without claiming any monetary charge. A refund replacement carries its actual newly credited value, without implying a new purchase/payment or additional bonus.

The v2 event deliberately carries no `amount`, `bonus_amount_minor` or `denomination_policy_version` purchase fields. Accurate charge/bonus/policy snapshots remain on Pricing's purchase-specific record and authorized service DTOs. Reward redemption identity and refund/replacement links also remain authoritative source records; Notification retrieves only the protected material needed for delivery. Do not fabricate purchase evidence for a reward or replacement.

## Source ownership and delivery

Pricing owns all three committed facts. Every producer must create a real immutable issuance identity and durable source association:

- `purchase`: existing captured gift purchase and its charge/bonus/policy evidence.
- `membership_reward`: fulfilled membership redemption and its awarded gift value.
- `refund_replacement`: committed gift refund restoration to a newly issued replacement, linked to its original instrument/refund.

Card code and PIN rules apply to every source: GC plus 12 decimal digits and a protected system-generated four-digit PIN retaining leading zeroes. Source business identifiers may remain deterministic idempotency keys; customer-facing gift codes must follow the canonical format and uniqueness rules. Existing GiftCard, reward outcome and ledger model shapes remain unchanged.

Pricing must atomically commit source entitlement/refund, new card/value ledger, protected issuance/credential data and outbox where required. Retry/replay must not create a second instrument or rotate credentials of a completed issuance. These transaction, generation and protection mechanisms are service-owned, not shared runtime code.

Notification consumes v2 on the existing customer-events route and resolves `issuance_id` through the authenticated, authorized Pricing-private lookup. That lookup must support all three sources, validate event/record identity, source, frozen initial value/currency and authoritative geography, and never require fabricated denomination/purchase fields for non-purchases. The delivery state machine/templates must distinguish owned reward/replacement cards from ownerless purchased gifts while using the real protected code/PIN. Source-specific private DTO changes belong to Backend. Raw credential lookup results must not enter events, public records, logs or tracing.

`notification.gift_card_delivered` remains **v1**, with only `{issuance_id, delivered_at}`. Its meaning is successful delivery for the correlated issuance, across all three sources. Orders completes digital purchase fulfilment only when the issuance actually belongs to that gift-purchase order and both issuance and delivery facts are satisfied. A delivered reward/replacement does not complete a purchase merely because it has a gift-card issuance ID. Claim remains independent of successful delivery.

## Compatibility and rollout

This is an additive Go-module minor release with an independently versioned breaking event representation. The v1 model remains unchanged: purchase-only, amount equals buyer charge, optional bonus/policy evidence. Never publish reward/replacement facts in that v1 shape. Current registry selects wallet.gift_card_issued v2; all other event versions remain unchanged, including payment.captured v2 and notification.gift_card_delivered v1.

Update module and CI pins to v38.2.0. Notification adopts v2 and the generalized private lookup/delivery route before Pricing emits it; Orders retains explicit purchase correlation. Avoid simultaneous duplicate v1/v2 publication for one issuance unless Backend establishes durable common issuance dedupe. This release itself does not deploy services, change transport permissions or prove live delivery.

No migrations, backfills, resets, database deletion or legacy conversion are supplied. Backend verifies purchase/reward/refund idempotency, code uniqueness, protected credential issuance, private-lookup authorization, Notification consumer versions/templates/retries and source-specific delivery before production rollout.

## Contract evidence

Tests cover exact v2 JSON/roundtrip for every source, required fields and optional geography, a closed safe field surface excluding PIN/card code/customer/contact/purchase claims, source enum validation, and unchanged v1 purchase serialization. Existing GiftCard reward outcomes and refund links remain intact; independent review covers value meaning, source correlation and compatibility.
